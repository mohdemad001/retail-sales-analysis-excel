#  Retail Sales Dashboard (Excel)

## Overview

Built an Excel-based dashboard to analyse retail sales data and generate business insights across products, regions, and time.

---

## Files

* `retail_sales_dataset.xlsx` – Source dataset
* `dashboard.pdf` – Final dashboard
* `product-analysis.png` – Product revenue contribution
* `revenue-by-region.png` – Revenue by region
* `revenue-trend.png` – Daily revenue trend

---

## What I Did

* Cleaned and structured raw sales data into an Excel table
* Built pivot tables to summarise revenue by product, region, and date
* Calculated % revenue contribution using `GETPIVOTDATA`
* Created charts (bar + line) for visual analysis
* Designed a clean dashboard combining KPIs and visuals

---

## Key Insights

* Laptop is the top-performing product (~36% of total revenue)
* South region generates the highest revenue
* Revenue shows frequent spikes indicating demand variability

---

## Dashboard

* KPIs: Total Revenue, Total Profit
* Product Analysis: % contribution by product
* Region Analysis: Revenue by region
* Trend: Revenue over time

---

## Skills Demonstrated

* Excel (Pivot Tables, Charts, GETPIVOTDATA)
* Data Analysis & Interpretation
* Dashboard Design
* Business Insight Communication
